Sec. 03 — Academic streams

Three routes into a technical career

Stream 01

Diploma & Polytechnic

Three-year diploma programs across engineering disciplines, delivered through affiliated government and private polytechnic colleges.

Stream 02

ITI & ITC

Industrial training at ITIs and ITCs, building skilled trades talent through hands-on, industry-aligned coursework.

Stream 03

RPETEAP

The state pre-entrance test for admission to engineering, architecture and pharmacy programs, held each May–June.

Sec. 04 — About the board

Raising technical manpower to a global standard

The Board affiliates and governs polytechnic colleges, ITIs and ITCs across Rajasthan — setting fee structures and syllabi, conducting examinations, and publishing results for every institute under its charge.

Its charter is straightforward: give the state's technical workforce training that holds up against any standard, in every district it reaches.
